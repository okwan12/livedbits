I landed in Osaka on a Tuesday and took the first train I could find north. The bloom line was supposed to hit Kyoto by Thursday, and I wanted to be standing under it when it did.

What I didn't expect was how loud a quiet city could be. Bicycle bells, vending machines humming on every corner, a shrine bell somewhere behind the trees. I spent the first morning just walking without a map, which is usually a mistake, but this time it wasn't.

By the third day I'd found a bench along the Philosopher's Path where the petals came down heavier than rain. I sat there for two hours. Nobody rushed me.
