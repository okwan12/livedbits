The fog never really left, and by the end I stopped minding. Some mornings you couldn't see past the second hill, and then by eleven it would burn off like nothing happened.

Six months of co-op, a lot of Muni delays, and one very good bagel place I found by accident in week two. I walked back to it on my last weekend just to have it one more time.

Packing up a temporary life is a strange kind of editing. What actually made the six months, once you take away the routine? Mostly: the walk over to the office on the clear days, the view from the office at golden hour, and the people who made the ordinary parts feel less ordinary.

Next stop: the East Coast, with a laptop full of half-finished projects and rolls I still need to develop.
